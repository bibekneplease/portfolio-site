Who I Am

I am an IT Infrastructure & Endpoint Support Engineer with 4+ years of experience across enterprise service desk, onsite field support, endpoint management and infrastructure operations. My background includes supporting large user and device environments, administering Microsoft workplace technologies and troubleshooting enterprise connectivity.

My experience includes Fujitsu Australia, an onsite deployment at Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE) through Black Box Technologies Australia, and earlier service-desk support at ZAMTAS. My technical work spans Active Directory, Group Policy, Microsoft 365, SCCM, ServiceNow, DNS/DHCP, VPN, RDP, VoIP, VMware and enterprise endpoint and data-centre support.

I focus on structured troubleshooting, clear incident documentation, controlled change practices and dependable service delivery across multi-site environments.
